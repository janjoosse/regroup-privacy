Regroup Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Regroup is a ski app for planning routes on the slopes with your group. It is made by Jan Joosse (“we”). This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what stays on your phone. The short version: we only store your account, your location never leaves your phone, and there are no ads, analytics or tracking.

Your account

When you create an account we store your name, email address and password. We use them to sign you in and so friends can recognise you in the app. Your password is stored hashed: we can't read it. Accounts are hosted by Supabase, which processes this data on our behalf.

Your location

If you allow location access, the app uses it only on your phone to show where you are, plan routes from there and tick off the lifts and runs you've done. Your location is not sent to us or stored on a server.

Stored only on your phone

Your home base, settings (map style, pace, runs to avoid), planned trips and downloaded ski areas are saved on your device and are removed when you delete the app.

Services the app connects to

  • Maps: map images come from MapTiler, Mapbox or OpenStreetMap. Like any website, they see your IP address and which part of the map is loaded.
  • Address search: when you search for your home base, the search text is sent to MapTiler to find it.
  • Ski areas: the list of ski areas and their maps are downloaded from our Supabase server. No personal data is sent for this.
  • Restaurant reviews: “Reviews” opens Google Maps' own page inside the app. Google's privacy policy applies there.
  • TestFlight: during the beta, Apple shares crash reports and the feedback you choose to send with us, under Apple's privacy policy.

What we don't do

No advertising, no analytics, no tracking across apps or websites, and we never sell or share your data with anyone other than the services above.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your account until you ask us to delete it. You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time. During the beta, the easiest private way is to send feedback from the TestFlight app (take a screenshot in Regroup, or open Regroup in TestFlight and tap “Send Beta Feedback”). We'll delete your account and reply within 30 days.

Children

Regroup is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above. For general questions, open an issue at github.com/janjoosse/regroup-privacy (issues are public, so please don't include personal details there).
